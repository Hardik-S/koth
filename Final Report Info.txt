As I am sure you are aware, you all have your final reports and videos due on the 9th. In case you are working on them or looking to polish some sections up here are some considerations that might be helpful:
0. Structure

    Abstract: A brief summary of the problem addressed, the proposed approach or method, key results, and the main contributions of the paper.
    Introduction: Introduces the research problem and its importance, outlines the research objectives and contributions, and provides a high-level overview of the paper’s structure.
    Background: Reviews relevant prior work, theories, and technical concepts needed to understand the study, and positions the paper within existing research.
    Methods: Describes the algorithms, models, datasets, experimental setup, and implementation details used to conduct the study, enabling reproducibility.
    Results: Presents the experimental or empirical findings, including quantitative evaluations, comparisons, and observed performance without interpretation beyond what is directly supported by the data.
    Conclusion: Summarizes the main contributions and findings, discusses their implications, acknowledges limitations, and suggests directions for future work.

1. Problem Definition and Context

    Is the problem clearly stated?

    Is it clear why this problem is interesting or important?

    Does the report explain:

        The goal of the analysis

        What a successful solution would look like

        Who might benefit from the results

2. Dataset Description

    Is the dataset described clearly and in sufficient detail?

    Does the report explain:

        Where the data came from

        What the key features are

        Any limitations or potential biases in the data

    Is it clear how the target variable is defined?

3. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing

    Were data‐quality issues discussed (missing values, outliers, formatting)?

    Were appropriate steps taken to clean the dataset?

    Is feature engineering explained and justified?

4. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

    Did the report include meaningful:

        Descriptive statistics

        Visualizations

        Interpretation of trends or relationships

    Is EDA used to motivate modeling decisions?

5. Modeling Approach

    Are the chosen classification models clearly identified?

    Does the report explain:

        Why those models were selected

        How hyperparameters were tuned (if applicable)

        The train/test or cross-validation strategy

    Is the modeling process reproducible and transparent?

6. Evaluation and Metrics

    Are appropriate evaluation metrics used (accuracy, F1, precision/recall, ROC-AUC, etc.)?

    Are the results interpreted correctly—beyond just listing numbers?

    If comparing multiple models:

        Is the comparison fair and well explained?

        Is the best model chosen with justification?

7. Interpretation and Insights

    Does the report explain what the model results mean in context?

    Are there meaningful conclusions, such as:

        Which factors drive predictions

        What stakeholders should take away

    Are limitations acknowledged?

8. Communication and Structure

    Is the report:

        Well organized

        Logically structured

        Clearly written

    Are figures and tables readable and properly labeled?

    Can someone not involved in the project understand your approach?

9. Technical Correctness

    Do the methods make sense statistically and computationally?

    Are arguments logically supported by evidence?

    Are any claims overstated or unjustified?

Hope this helps, and good luck!
